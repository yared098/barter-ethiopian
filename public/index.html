FAQ

Questions? Answered.

Yes — you can use Barter Ethio completely free forever. Free users can post 1 item per day and see the top 3 matches. If you want more posts or full match visibility, you can buy a plan anytime.

Barter is a direct swap: you offer a specific item and ask for a specific item in return. Both people must agree. Bidding is an auction: your item is listed for a period, multiple people bid (with cash, barter items, or both), and you pick the winner.

All payments go through Chapa, Ethiopia's trusted payment platform. You can pay with Telebirr, CBE Birr, Awash, Amole, or any Visa/Mastercard. Your credits activate instantly after payment.

Yes! Barter Ethio supports both Amharic and English. Switch anytime from the login screen or settings — the app remembers your choice.

Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We never share your information with third parties. Payment details are handled entirely by Chapa — we never see your card number or wallet PIN.

We recommend meeting in public places and inspecting items before swapping. If a dispute arises, contact our team at fdessalew@gmail.com — we can help mediate.
